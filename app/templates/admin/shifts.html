Shifts and hours

Two settings decide what gets paid: the shift sets the daily paid band, and the standard week sets how many hours in a Monday–Sunday week are paid at standard rate before overtime starts.

Shifts

Clocking in early pays from the shift start. Pay is counted in 15-minute steps — a 07:34 arrival is paid from 07:45.

Standard working weeks

Hours worked in a Monday–Sunday week beyond the standard week are reported as overtime. Someone on a 40-hour week who works 65 paid hours has 40 standard and 25 overtime.

An end time at or before the start time means the shift runs overnight. The unpaid break is only deducted once someone's paid time exceeds the "deduct break only after" threshold — so a short morning or afternoon stint keeps its full hours. Set the threshold to 0 to always deduct it.

Leave pay time worked after the shift end ticked so a late finish is paid and shows up as overtime once the standard week is used up. Untick it where staying on is not authorised work: pay is then trimmed back to the shift end. An early arrival is always paid from the shift start either way — waiting for the day to begin is not overtime.
